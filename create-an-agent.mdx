## **Overview**

The agent setup survey is the single form you complete after subscribing. It gives your Automation Expert everything they need to build, voice, and configure your agent — without you needing to touch any settings in your voice workspace.

You'll find the survey at `app.mavera.ai`. It takes around 5–10 minutes to complete.

![Agent Setup Survey Overview](/images/agent-setup-overview-placeholder.png)

---

## **Before You Start**

Make sure you have:

- An active Mavera AI subscription and access to your `voice.mavera.ai` account
- The name you want your AI agent to use
- A list of the services your business offers (and prices, if relevant)
- Your business hours and location
- A rough idea of the voice and personality you want your agent to have

---

## **Section 1: Your Details**

Provide your contact information so your Automation Expert can reach you during setup:

- **First and last name**
- **Phone number**
- **Email address**

This is also the email tied to your `voice.mavera.ai` account.

---

## **Section 2: Your Agent**

### **Agent Name**

Choose a name for your AI assistant. This is the name your agent will use when introducing itself to callers — for example, *"Hi, I'm Maya from Acme Plumbing."*

Keep it natural and on-brand. It doesn't have to be your business name.

### **Agent Description** *(optional)*

Use this field to give your Automation Expert any specific context about how you want your agent to behave — for example:

- *"Always ask for a callback number early in the call"*
- *"Focus on booking consultations, not quoting over the phone"*
- *"Avoid mentioning competitors"*

![Agent Details Section](/images/agent-setup-details-placeholder.png)

---

## **Section 3: Your Services**

List the services your business offers. Your agent will use this list to answer questions, qualify leads, and guide conversations.

- **Services** — what you do (e.g. boiler repair, dental hygiene appointments, conveyancing)
- **Service prices** *(optional)* — if you want your agent to quote prices over the phone

Be as specific as you need. Your Automation Expert will use this to write the agent's knowledge of your business.

---

## **Section 4: Working Days and Hours**

Tell us when your agent should be active:

- **Working days** — which days of the week your agent handles calls
- **Working hours** — start and end times for each day

Your agent can be set to handle calls 24/7 or within specific hours only. Outside of working hours, calls can be handled differently — discuss your preference in the description field.

---

## **Section 5: Agent Voice**

This is how your agent sounds. Choose from the following options:

### **Gender**
- Male
- Female

### **Accent**
- British (UK)
- American (US)
- Australian
- Other regional accents available — mention in the description field

### **Tone**
- Professional
- Friendly
- Energetic
- Calm and reassuring

### **Personality**
- Concise and direct
- Warm and conversational
- Informative and thorough
- Confident and persuasive

These choices feed directly into voice selection and prompt style. Your Automation Expert may follow up with voice samples before finalising.

![Voice Preference Section](/images/agent-setup-voice-placeholder.png)

---

## **Section 6: Business Location**

Enter your operating location or service area. This helps your agent answer questions like *"Do you cover my area?"* and is used in the agent's context prompt.

---

## **Submitting the Survey**

When you submit the form:

1. Your setup is recorded and assigned to an Automation Expert
2. You receive a confirmation email acknowledging your submission
3. Your Account Manager ensures work begins within the first 3 hours of sign-up
4. You'll be notified when your agent is ready for final testing

![Survey Submission Confirmation](/images/agent-setup-submitted-placeholder.png)

---

## **What Happens After Submission**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agent Built from Template">
    Your Automation Expert imports your first agent from a template in `voice.mavera.ai` and adapts it to your business using the survey responses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prompts Customised">
    All prompts are rewritten to reflect your services, brand voice, tone, and working hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voice Assigned">
    The right voice profile is selected and applied to your agent based on your gender, accent, tone, and personality preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Telephony Connected">
    A phone number is purchased and configured, Twilio is connected to your workspace, and call routing is set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrations Attached">
    Calendars, CRM connections, and any other integrations you need are added and tested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="QA and Handover">
    Your Account Manager runs a stress test, then shares the agent with you for final approval before going live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my answers after submitting?">
    Yes — contact [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai) or your Account Manager before your agent goes live. Once live, changes can be requested at any time and will be handled by your Automation Expert.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I have more than one agent?">
    Your first agent is built as part of your standard onboarding. Additional agents can be requested via your Account Manager. Each agent can have different services, voices, and behaviours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my agent handle both inbound and outbound calls?">
    Yes, on the Professional and Enterprise plans. Inbound call handling is included on all plans. Outbound calling with reminder campaigns is available from Professional onwards.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does my agent need to match the language of my customers?">
    Your agent is configured in the language you specify. For businesses with multilingual customers, speak to your Account Manager — additional language configurations may be available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
