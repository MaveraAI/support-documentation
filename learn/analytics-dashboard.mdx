## **Overview**

The Analytics Dashboard in `voice.mavera.ai` gives you a high-level view of how your agent is performing — call volumes, durations, outcomes, and trends over time. It's the fastest way to understand whether your agent is doing its job and where there's room to improve.

No setup required. Analytics are populated automatically as your agent handles calls.

![Analytics Dashboard Overview](/images/analytics-overview-placeholder.png)

---

## **Key Metrics**

### **Call Volume**
- **Total calls** — how many calls your agent has handled in a given period
- **Inbound vs outbound** — breakdown by call direction
- **Calls by day / week / month** — volume trends over time
- **Peak hours** — when your agent is busiest

### **Call Outcomes**
- **Completed** — calls that reached a natural end
- **Transferred** — calls handed off to a human
- **Dropped** — calls that ended unexpectedly
- **Voicemail** — calls that went to voicemail (outbound)
- **No answer** — outbound calls not answered

### **Call Duration**
- **Average call length** — useful for spotting conversations that are too long or too short
- **Duration distribution** — how call lengths spread across short, medium, and long

### **Action Performance** *(where applicable)*
- **Bookings made** — calendar appointments created during calls
- **CRM records logged** — contacts or leads created
- **Payment events** *(Professional and Enterprise)* — payment actions triggered

## **Reading the Data**

### **High drop rate**
If a significant portion of calls are being dropped (caller hangs up before a natural end), this may indicate the agent's opening isn't landing well, or the caller isn't getting to the information they need quickly enough. Share this with your Account Manager.

### **Long average call duration**
Longer calls aren't always better. If calls are consistently longer than expected, your agent may be giving overly verbose responses, or callers may be struggling to get what they need. Review the [Logs](/learn/logs) for context.

### **Low booking conversion** *(if calendar integrated)*
If many calls include a discussion of appointments but few result in a booking, the agent's booking flow may need adjustment. Flag this to your Account Manager.

### **Volume spikes**
A sudden increase in call volume may indicate a campaign, a viral moment, or a change in demand. Ensure your concurrent call limits are sufficient — see [Telephony](/launch/telephony).

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How often is the analytics data updated?">
    Call data is reflected in the dashboard in near real time — typically within a few minutes of each call ending.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export analytics data?">
    Yes. See [Export Call Data](/learn/export-call-data) for options on downloading your data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see analytics for individual agents?">
    Yes — if you have multiple agents, you can filter the dashboard by agent to see performance data separately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What counts as a successful call?">
    A completed call is one that reached a natural end without being dropped. Whether it was "successful" in business terms (e.g. a booking made) depends on the actions configured in your agent. These are tracked separately under Action Performance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
