## **Overview**

Every call your agent handles is logged in full inside your `voice.mavera.ai` workspace. Logs give you a complete record of what was said, what the agent did, and how the conversation ended — conversation by conversation, in real time.

You don't need to set anything up. Logging is on by default for all accounts.

![Logs Overview](/images/logs-overview-placeholder.png)

---

## **Accessing Your Logs**

1. Log in to your `voice.mavera.ai` workspace
2. Navigate to **Logs** in the left sidebar
3. Browse or search your call history
4. Click any call to view the full transcript and details

![Logs Interface](/images/logs-interface-placeholder.png)

---

## **What's in a Log Entry**

Every logged call contains:

### **Call Information**
- Date and time of the call
- Call direction (inbound or outbound)
- Phone number dialled / caller's number
- Call duration
- Call outcome (completed, dropped, transferred, voicemail)

### **Full Transcript**
A line-by-line transcript of everything said — both the caller and your agent. Transcripts are generated automatically after each call and are typically ready within a minute of the call ending.

### **Call Recording**
A full audio recording of the call, playable directly in the logs interface.

| Plan | Recording Storage |
|---|---|
| **Pay As You Go** | 7 days |
| **Professional / Enterprise** | 2 years |

### **Actions Executed**
If your agent triggered any actions during the call (e.g. created a calendar booking, logged a CRM record), these are listed in the log entry alongside the result.

### **Variables and Context**
Key data points collected during the call — caller name, service enquiry, booking details, outcome — stored alongside the transcript.

## **Filtering and Searching**

Use the filter bar to narrow down your call history:

- **Date range** — find calls from a specific period
- **Call direction** — inbound or outbound only
- **Call duration** — filter by shorter or longer calls
- **Outcome** — completed, transferred, dropped, voicemail
- **Keyword search** — search transcripts for specific words or phrases (e.g. a customer name, a service, a postcode)

## **Using Logs to Improve Your Agent**

Logs are the best tool for identifying what's working and what needs improvement. Useful things to look for:

- **Short calls with no outcome** — may indicate the agent isn't understanding the caller correctly
- **Repeated questions your agent can't answer** — content that should be added to the [knowledge base](/build/knowledge-base)
- **Calls transferred or dropped** — may indicate a flow issue or missing integration
- **Caller frustration** — detectable in the transcript by repeated questions or caller asking to speak to a human

Share any patterns you spot with your Account Manager and your Automation Expert will make the necessary adjustments.

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How soon after a call is the transcript available?">
    Transcripts are typically ready within 60 seconds of the call ending. For longer calls, it may take a few minutes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are all calls recorded?">
    Yes, all calls are recorded by default. Recordings are stored for 7 days on Pay As You Go and 2 years on Professional and Enterprise.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I search for a specific caller's transcript?">
    Yes — use the keyword search to find calls by phone number, caller name (if collected), or any words that appeared in the transcript.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share a call log with my team?">
    You can export call data or share access to your `voice.mavera.ai` workspace with additional team members. Contact your Account Manager to set up additional workspace users.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if a call is missing from the logs?">
    If you believe a call was handled by your agent but it doesn't appear in logs, contact [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai) with the date, time, and number. We'll investigate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
