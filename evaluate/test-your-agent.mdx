## **Overview**

Before your agent handles a real customer call, it goes through multiple rounds of testing — by your Automation Expert, your Account Manager, and finally by you. This page explains what testing looks like at each stage and how to get the most out of your final review.

![Testing Overview](/images/testing-overview-placeholder.png)

---

## **Who Tests What**

| Stage | Who tests | What's tested |
|---|---|---|
| First-round testing | Automation Expert | Core call flow, voice, integrations |
| QA stress test | Account Manager | Edge cases, unusual caller behaviour, failure handling |
| Final client testing | You | Your real scenarios, your judgement |

Your agent only goes live once you've given sign-off. You're not rushed through this stage.

---

## **How to Test Your Agent**

When your Account Manager notifies you that your agent is ready for final testing, you'll receive your agent's phone number. The best way to test it is simple: **call it**.

Use a real phone — don't use VoIP or web calling tools if you can avoid it, as they don't always reproduce the call quality your customers will experience.

### **What to test:**

**Cover your most common scenarios first:**
- A customer enquiring about your main service
- A customer asking about pricing
- A customer wanting to book an appointment
- A customer asking about your location or hours

**Then test edge cases:**
- A caller who's vague or unclear about what they want
- A caller who asks something your agent shouldn't know (to check how it handles uncertainty)
- A caller who asks to speak to a human
- A caller who calls outside business hours (if you have a test number that supports this)

**Pay attention to:**
- Does the agent introduce itself correctly?
- Does it sound natural — not robotic or stilted?
- Does it handle your services accurately?
- Does it pick up the right intent from how callers phrase things?
- Does it complete the full flow (e.g. actually create a booking if that's configured)?
- Does the voice suit your brand?

## **Reviewing Test Calls in Logs**

Every test call is logged in your `voice.mavera.ai` workspace exactly the same as a live call. After your test calls, log in to [Logs](/learn/logs) and review:

- The full transcript of your conversation
- Whether any actions were executed correctly
- How long the call took
- Whether anything was unexpected or off

This is useful for spotting issues that are hard to notice mid-call — phrasing that sounds slightly wrong in writing, or a step in the flow that was skipped.

## **Giving Feedback**

If you find anything during testing that you want changed, write it down specifically and share it with your Account Manager. Useful feedback sounds like:

- *"When I asked about pricing, it gave the wrong figure — it said £150 but the correct price is £175"*
- *"The greeting feels too formal for our business — can we make it more relaxed?"*
- *"It didn't ask for my name before trying to book the appointment"*
- *"When I said I wasn't sure what I needed, it didn't offer to help me figure it out — it just listed services"*

Vague feedback like *"it didn't sound right"* is harder to action. The more specific you are, the faster your Automation Expert can make targeted adjustments.

Your Account Manager will coordinate the changes, your Automation Expert will update the agent, and you'll be invited to test again.

---

## **Testing After Changes**

Any time your agent is updated — new service added, prompt adjusted, integration changed — it should be tested again before being relied on for live calls. Your Account Manager will coordinate this whenever a significant change is made.

For minor updates (e.g. a small pricing change in the knowledge base), your Account Manager will advise whether a full round of testing is needed or whether a spot-check is sufficient.

---

## **Simulations** *(Advanced)*

In addition to live test calls, your `voice.mavera.ai` workspace supports simulated conversations — automated tests that run predefined call scenarios against your agent without making a real phone call. These are used internally by your Automation Expert during the build phase.

If you want to set up custom simulation scenarios for ongoing regression testing, speak to your Account Manager.

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I test without making a real phone call?">
    The best test is always a real call, because it replicates the actual caller experience including voice quality, latency, and natural speech. For functional testing (e.g. checking that a booking action works), your Automation Expert can run simulations during the build phase.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I find a bug after the agent goes live?">
    Contact your Account Manager immediately. For issues that affect callers, they can temporarily suspend the agent while it's fixed. For minor issues, a fix can be deployed without interruption.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many rounds of testing are normal?">
    Most agents are ready after one or two rounds of client testing. If your business is complex or you have specific edge cases you want to cover, more rounds are fine — the goal is that you're genuinely happy before going live.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I invite someone else from my team to test?">
    Yes. Give them your agent's test number and ask them to call and report back. This is a good way to get a second opinion, especially from someone who wasn't involved in the setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will test calls appear in my call logs?">
    Yes. All calls — including test calls — appear in your logs. You can identify them by date (they'll be before your go-live date) or by the phone number used.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
