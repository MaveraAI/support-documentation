## **Overview**

Your agent's knowledge base is the information it draws on during calls to answer questions about your business. Without it, your agent can only work from what's in its prompt. With a well-populated knowledge base, it can accurately answer specific questions about your services, pricing, policies, and processes — without guessing.

Mavera's Automation Experts populate and configure the knowledge base for you as part of onboarding. You provide the source material; they upload it, structure it, and make sure your agent uses it correctly.

---

## **What Your Agent Can Know**

The knowledge base is the right place for any information your agent should reference during a call but that's too detailed or changeable to hardcode into a prompt:

- **Service details** — full descriptions of what you offer
- **Pricing** — service fees, packages, deposit amounts
- **FAQs** — common customer questions and accurate answers
- **Policies** — cancellation, refunds, terms of service
- **Coverage areas** — postcodes, regions, locations you serve
- **Business details** — opening hours, parking, directions, team members
- **Product information** — specifications, comparisons, requirements

---

## **What You Need to Provide**

Send your source material to your Automation Expert or Account Manager in any of the following formats:

**Supported formats:**
- PDF (`.pdf`)
- Word documents (`.docx`, `.doc`)
- Plain text (`.txt`)
- Spreadsheets (`.csv`) — useful for pricing tables
- Website URLs — your Automation Expert can scrape relevant pages
- Direct text — paste content directly into a message or email

You don't need to format it in any particular way. Your Automation Expert will extract the relevant content and structure it for use in your agent's workspace.

## **How the Knowledge Base Works in Calls**

When a caller asks a question your agent doesn't know from its prompt alone, it searches the knowledge base in real time and uses the most relevant information to form its answer. For example:

- *"Do you cover Birmingham?"* → agent checks your coverage area list
- *"How much does a boiler service cost?"* → agent retrieves your pricing
- *"What's your cancellation policy?"* → agent reads from your policy document

The agent won't make up answers — if the information isn't in the knowledge base or prompt, it will tell the caller it doesn't know and offer to take a message or transfer the call.

---

## **Keeping the Knowledge Base Up to Date**

Your knowledge base should reflect your current services, pricing, and policies at all times. If something changes — new services added, prices updated, policies revised — you need to let your Account Manager know so the knowledge base can be updated accordingly.

**To update your knowledge base:**
1. Send the new or updated content to [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai) or your Account Manager
2. Your Automation Expert will update the knowledge base in `voice.mavera.ai`
3. The changes take effect on the next call

## **Best Practices**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be specific and complete">
    Vague content produces vague answers. Instead of "we offer cleaning services", give your agent the full list: what types of cleaning, what's included, how long each takes, and the price range.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Include your most common caller questions">
    Think about the questions your reception team answers every day. These are the exact questions your agent will face — make sure the answers are in the knowledge base.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep content current">
    An outdated price or discontinued service in the knowledge base will cause your agent to give wrong information. Review your knowledge base content whenever you make a business change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Don't duplicate information">
    If the same information is in both the prompt and the knowledge base, there's potential for conflict. Your Automation Expert will manage this — just provide accurate source material.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I upload documents myself?">
    No. You send your source material to your Automation Expert or Account Manager and they handle the upload and configuration in `voice.mavera.ai`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I include my website as a source?">
    Yes. If your website contains accurate, up-to-date information about your services and business, your Automation Expert can scrape it as part of knowledge base setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the agent decide which information to use?">
    The knowledge base uses semantic search — when a caller asks a question, the agent finds the most relevant sections of your documents and uses them to construct an answer. The relevance threshold and number of sources used are configured by your Automation Expert.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the caller asks something not in the knowledge base?">
    Your agent will acknowledge it doesn't have that information and offer to take a message, transfer the call, or advise the caller to contact you directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much content can I include?">
    There's no strict document limit. Your Automation Expert will advise if the volume of content needs to be structured or prioritised in any particular way.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
