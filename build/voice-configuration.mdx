## **Overview**

Your agent's voice is one of the most important parts of how your brand comes across on a call. Mavera AI configures the voice for you — based on your preferences from the [agent setup survey](/create-an-agent) — using the voice engine inside `voice.mavera.ai`.

You don't need to configure voice settings yourself. Your Automation Expert selects and applies the right voice, and your Account Manager will confirm the choice with you before going live.

## **Voice Preferences You Provide**

During the agent setup survey, you specify four voice preferences. These are used together to find the closest matching voice:

### **Gender**
- **Male** — deeper, typically more authoritative
- **Female** — typically perceived as warmer and more approachable

### **Accent**
- **British (UK)** — standard or regional British accent
- **American (US)** — neutral or regional US accent
- **Australian** — Australian accent
- Other regional accents can be requested via the description field in the survey

### **Tone**
| Tone | Description | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Professional** | Clear, formal, measured delivery | B2B, legal, financial |
| **Friendly** | Warm, approachable, conversational | Customer service, hospitality |
| **Energetic** | Upbeat, fast-paced, enthusiastic | Sales, promotions |
| **Calm and reassuring** | Slow, steady, empathetic | Healthcare, support |

### **Personality**
| Personality | Description |
|---|---|
| **Concise and direct** | Gets to the point quickly, minimal filler |
| **Warm and conversational** | Natural, human-feeling flow |
| **Informative and thorough** | Detailed explanations, covers all bases |
| **Confident and persuasive** | Assertive, clear calls to action |

## **How Voice Assignment Works**

Once your survey is submitted, your Automation Expert:

1. Matches your preferences to available voice profiles on `voice.mavera.ai`
2. Applies the voice to your agent configuration
3. Adjusts speaking rate and pacing to suit your use case
4. Tests the voice across typical call scenarios

If no single voice precisely matches all your preferences, your Automation Expert will select the closest option and may share samples with you for final sign-off before launch.

---

## **Custom Voice** *(Professional and Enterprise)*

On the Professional and Enterprise plans, a custom voice can be configured — a voice that is distinct and consistent with your brand identity. This goes beyond the standard preset voices and is set up as part of your onboarding.

To request a custom voice, include this in the description field of your agent setup survey or contact your Account Manager.

---

## **Multilingual Agents**

Your agent can be configured in any of the supported languages available on your plan. On the Pay As You Go plan, agents are configured in English. The Professional and Enterprise plans support over 100 languages.

If you serve customers who speak multiple languages, speak to your Account Manager — separate agents can be configured per language, each with their own voice and prompt set.

---

## **Voice and Prompt Consistency**

Your agent's voice is only one half of how it sounds. The other half is the prompt — the instructions that govern how it speaks, what it says, and how it responds. Your Automation Expert writes the prompt to match the personality and tone you selected, so the written style and the voice work together as a coherent whole.

---

## **Requesting Voice Changes**

If you want to change your agent's voice after going live:

1. Contact your Account Manager or email [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai)
2. Describe the change you'd like (e.g. switch from friendly to professional tone, try a different accent)
3. Your Automation Expert will update the voice configuration and share samples before re-deploying

We recommend testing voice changes in [simulations](/evaluate/simulations) before applying them to your live agent.

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I hear the voice before my agent goes live?">
    Yes. Your Account Manager will arrange for you to test-call your agent during the final testing phase before launch. If you're not happy with the voice at that point, your Automation Expert will adjust it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do advanced voice tweaks sometimes affect quality?">
    Voice synthesis has natural trade-offs — adjusting pitch, pace, or style can occasionally introduce artefacts or reduce naturalness. Your Automation Expert will flag these during testing and find the best balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the voice need to match the language?">
    Yes — for the best caller experience, the voice and language should match. If you need agents in multiple languages, each agent should be configured with a voice native to that language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is a custom voice available on all plans?">
    Custom voice is available on the Professional and Enterprise plans. Pay As You Go plans use standard preset voices.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
