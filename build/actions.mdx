## **Overview**

Actions are the integrations your agent uses during a live call to actually do things — not just talk. When a caller wants to book an appointment, your agent can check availability and create the booking in real time. When a lead comes in, your agent can log it directly to your CRM.

Mavera's Automation Experts configure all actions for you as part of onboarding. You don't set these up yourself — you describe what you need in the [agent setup survey](/create-an-agent) and your Automation Expert connects the right systems.

## **What Actions Are Available**

### **Calendar Booking**

Your agent can check real-time availability and book appointments directly into your calendar during the call — no follow-up needed.

- **Pay As You Go**: 1 calendar integration
- **Professional / Enterprise**: Unlimited calendars

Supported calendar providers include Google Calendar, Outlook, and Calendly. If you use a different provider, speak to your Account Manager.

**What your agent can do:**
- Check available slots
- Book new appointments
- Collect caller details for the booking
- Send confirmation messages after the call

![Calendar Action](/images/actions-calendar-placeholder.png)

---

### **CRM Integration**

Your agent automatically logs every qualified lead and interaction to your CRM — no manual data entry.

- **Pay As You Go**: Salesforce only
- **Professional / Enterprise**: All major CRMs

Supported CRMs include Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Zoho. Enterprise plans support custom CRM integrations.

**What gets logged:**
- Caller name and contact details
- Service enquiry or intent
- Outcome of the call (booked, interested, callback requested, etc.)
- Timestamp and call duration

---

### **Payment Processing** *(Professional and Enterprise)*

Your agent can take payments over the phone — useful for deposits, booking fees, or pay-as-you-go services.

Supported payment providers: **Stripe**, **PayPal**

Your Automation Expert will configure the payment flow as part of your onboarding. Speak to your Account Manager about compliance and PCI considerations before enabling this.

---

### **Outbound Reminder Campaigns** *(Professional and Enterprise)*

Your agent can make outbound calls — not just receive them. This is used for appointment reminders, follow-ups, and re-engagement campaigns.

Campaigns are set up and managed by your Automation Expert and Account Manager. Contact [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai) to discuss a campaign.

---

### **Custom HTTP Actions** *(Professional and Enterprise)*

For businesses with custom systems, your agent can call any external API during a call. This allows integration with bespoke booking systems, inventory databases, custom CRMs, or any internal tooling.

Speak to your Account Manager to scope a custom integration.

---

## **How Actions Are Set Up**

You don't configure actions through a dashboard — your Automation Expert sets them up directly in `voice.mavera.ai` using the information you provide. The process is:

1. **You describe what you need** in the agent setup survey or via your Account Manager
2. **Your Automation Expert connects the integration** — they configure the API credentials, map the fields, and test the action end-to-end
3. **Testing and sign-off** — your Account Manager verifies the integration works correctly in QA before going live

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to provide API credentials for integrations?">
    Yes — for CRM and calendar integrations, you'll need to share API keys or OAuth credentials with your Automation Expert so they can connect your systems. Your Account Manager will guide you through this step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my agent check calendar availability in real time?">
    Yes. Your agent queries the connected calendar live during the call and can only offer slots that are genuinely available at that moment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What CRMs are supported?">
    On the Professional and Enterprise plans: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, and others. Enterprise plans also support custom integrations. Pay As You Go is limited to Salesforce.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my agent send a confirmation after booking?">
    Yes — confirmation messages (SMS or email) can be sent automatically after a successful booking. This is configured by your Automation Expert as part of the action setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add a new integration after going live?">
    Contact your Account Manager or email [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai). Your Automation Expert will add and test the new action before it goes live.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
