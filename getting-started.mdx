## **What is Mavera AI?**

Mavera AI is a fully managed AI voice agent service. You don't configure anything yourself — our team of Automation Experts builds, tests, and launches your agent for you. You provide the details about your business and agent, and we handle everything on `voice.mavera.ai`, the platform that powers your AI.

Under the hood, every Mavera account runs on a dedicated voice workspace. Your agents, phone numbers, call logs, and analytics all live there — accessible to you at any time.

## **Step 1: Choose Your Plan and Sign Up**

Visit [mavera.ai/packages](https://mavera.ai/packages) and select the plan that fits your business:

| Plan | Price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pay As You Go** | £0/month \+ usage | Getting started, low call volume |
| **Professional** | £999/month | Growing businesses, full feature set |
| **Enterprise** | From £2,000/month | High volume, custom requirements |

Once you select a plan, you'll be prompted to create your account:

- **Account Name** — the name of your business workspace (checked for availability in real time)
- **Email Address** — used to access `voice.mavera.ai`
- **Maverick Email** _(optional)_ — if a Mavera team member helped you sign up, enter their email here

After completing payment via Stripe, your dedicated voice workspace is automatically provisioned. You'll receive an email with your sign-in link to `voice.mavera.ai`.

## **Step 2: Complete the Agent Setup Survey**

Once your account is active, you'll be directed to the agent setup survey on `app.mavera.ai`. This is how you tell us about your business and the agent you want built.

The survey covers:

- **Your details** — name, phone, email
- **Agent name** — what your AI assistant will be called
- **Services** — what your business offers, including any service prices
- **Working days and hours** — when your agent should be active
- **Agent voice** — gender, accent, tone, and personality preferences
- **Business location** — your operating area
- **Agent description** — any specific behaviours or instructions for your agent

This information is handed directly to your Automation Expert to build your agent. You only need to fill it in once.

![Agent Setup Survey](/images/getting-started-survey-placeholder.png)

---

## **Step 3: Your Automation Expert Takes Over**

After you submit the survey, a Mavera Automation Expert is assigned to your account. They will:

1. Import your first agent from a template on `voice.mavera.ai`
2. Adapt all prompts to your business, brand, and services
3. Connect your phone number (via Twilio)
4. Assign the right voice to your agent
5. Attach calendars, CRM connections, or other integrations you've requested
6. Assign your phone number and complete the first round of testing

Your Account Manager then carries out a quality assurance stress test before sharing the agent with you for final sign-off.

**You will be notified at each key stage.** The full process — from survey submission to going live — typically completes within the first 24–72 hours.

![Onboarding Handover](/images/getting-started-handover-placeholder.png)

---

## **Step 4: Final Testing and Go-Live**

Before your agent goes live, your Account Manager will share it with you for final testing. You'll be able to:

- Call your agent directly to hear it in action
- Request any adjustments before launch
- Confirm you're happy with the voice, tone, and behaviour

Once approved, your agent is live and handling real calls.

## **FAQ**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to configure anything myself?">
    No. Mavera AI is a fully managed service. Your Automation Expert configures everything on your behalf, based on the agent setup survey you complete after signing up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is voice.mavera.ai?">
    `voice.mavera.ai` is your AI voice platform — where your agents run, your calls are logged, and your analytics live. You receive a sign-in link to access it after subscribing. Your Automation Expert also works here to build and tune your agents.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is app.mavera.ai?">
    `app.mavera.ai` is the Mavera management layer where you complete the agent setup survey and where our internal team tracks your onboarding progress and messaging configurations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    The full onboarding process — from survey completion to a tested, live agent — typically takes 24–72 hours. Your Account Manager will keep you updated throughout.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I want to change my agent after it's live?">
    Contact your Account Manager or email [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai) to request changes. Your Automation Expert will update and re-test your agent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my agent reaches voicemail?">
    Your agent is configured to detect voicemail and handle it gracefully — either leaving a message or hanging up, based on your preferences. You can discuss your preferred behaviour during the setup survey.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I buy phone numbers for different regions?">
    Yes. Mavera supports local, toll-free, and international numbers across multiple regions including the UK, US, Australia, and more. See [Telephony](/launch/telephony) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do subaccounts get their own minutes?">
    Each account has its own usage allocation based on your plan. Contact [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai) for details on usage and limits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When does billing occur?">
    Billing occurs on the same date each month from when you first subscribed, processed via Stripe. Contact [support@mavera.ai](mailto:support@mavera.ai) for billing enquiries.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
